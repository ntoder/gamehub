Gem Swap

Swap two gems next to each other to make a line of 3 or more.
Match 4 for a ⚡ striped gem and 5 for a 🌈 star gem!
You have 30 moves. Chain reactions score extra.

Out of Moves

Click a gem then a neighbour, or drag a gem to swap it. Stuck? A hint glows after a few seconds.

About Gem Swap

A colourful match-three puzzle. Swap two gems that sit next to each other to line up three or more of the same colour, and they burst into sparkles. New gems fall from the top, and if they make new lines you get a chain reaction worth extra points. Match four for a striped gem that clears a whole row, or five for a rainbow star that clears every gem of one colour. You have thirty moves to score as much as you can.

How to play

Click two gemsSwap them
Drag a gemSwap it with its neighbour

Tips

  • Make matches near the bottom. Falling gems often make free chain reactions.
  • Save the star gem and swap it with the colour there is most of.
  • Always look for a move that makes a line of four or five.
