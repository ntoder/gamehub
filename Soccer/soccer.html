About Soccer

A fast top-down football match against the computer, with a goalkeeper on each side. Dribble the ball by running into it, hold Space to charge a powerful shot, and score in the goal on the right. Pick easy, normal or hard, and whoever has more goals when the 90 seconds run out wins.

How to play

Arrow keys / WASDRun
ShiftSprint
Hold and release SpaceCharge and shoot

Tips

  • Get behind the ball before you shoot so it goes toward the goal.
  • A fully charged shot aimed at a corner is very hard to save.
  • Fall back to defend after you shoot. The computer is quick on the break.
