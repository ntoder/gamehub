How to get better at 2048: 6 tips that actually work

Noam's Game Hub · 4 November 2026 · 4 min read

2048 looks like a game of luck, but good players win again and again. The difference is not speed. It's a few simple habits that keep the board tidy, so you never run out of moves. Here are the six that matter most.

1. Pick a corner and stay there

Choose one corner (bottom-left is popular) and keep your biggest tile in it for the whole game. Every move should push tiles towards that corner. If your big tile leaves the corner, a small tile can slip in behind it, and that small tile is very hard to get rid of.

2. Use only three directions

If your corner is bottom-left, your main moves are down and left. Use right when you have to. Try never to press up, because it pulls your big tile out of its corner. Press it only when no other move is possible.

3. Build a "snake" of numbers

Line up your tiles in order along the bottom row: 256, 128, 64, 32… then wrap around into the row above. When the numbers get smaller as they move away from the corner, a single merge can start a chain reaction that combines everything into your corner tile.

4. Keep the bottom row full

If the row next to your corner has a gap, pressing left or down can move your big tile out of place. A full bottom row "locks" it in place, so you can slide the other rows freely.

5. Think one move ahead

Before each move, ask: where will the new tile appear? New tiles drop into empty squares, so after you move, there is a gap somewhere. Try to make that gap appear far away from your corner, where a stray 2 or 4 does less damage.

6. Don't chase small merges

It's tempting to merge two 4s the moment you see them, but if it means pressing the wrong direction, wait. Small tiles can wait. Your corner tile can't.

Bonus: use Undo to learn

Our version of 2048 has an Undo button. Use it to try a move, see what happens, and go back. It's the fastest way to learn which moves are safe. When you're ready for a real challenge, play a game without undo and see how far you get.

Good luck! A score of 10,000 earns the Number cruncher achievement on your profile.
