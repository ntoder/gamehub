About Mine Finder

A logic puzzle where every number tells you how many hidden mines touch that square. Use the numbers to work out which squares are safe, open them, and put flags on the mines. Your first click is always safe. Clear the whole board as fast as you can: the quicker you finish, the more points you get.

How to play

ClickOpen a square
Right-click / long-pressPlace or remove a flag
Click a numberOpen all its unflagged neighbours

Tips

  • Start by looking at the 1s near the edges. They are the easiest to solve.
  • If a number already has all its mines flagged, every other square around it is safe.
  • Use flag mode on a phone so a tap places a flag.
