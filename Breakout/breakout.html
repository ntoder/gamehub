About Brick Smash

Bounce the ball off your paddle and break every brick on the screen. Where the ball hits the paddle decides its angle, so you can aim. Gold bricks need two hits, and a falling star makes your paddle extra wide for a while. Clear the wall to move to a harder level with a faster ball.

How to play

Mouse / fingerMove the paddle
Arrow keysMove the paddle
Click / SpaceLaunch the ball

Tips

  • Hit the ball with the edge of the paddle to send it sideways.
  • Break a hole through to the top and let the ball bounce around up there.
  • Catch the star power-up: a wider paddle makes the fast levels much easier.
