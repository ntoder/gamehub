About Pixel Jumper

A colourful 3D platform adventure. Run and jump across the level, stomp on enemies, collect coins and reach the flag at the end to clear the course. You have three lives, so be careful near the gaps.

How to play

Arrow keys / joystickMove
Up / A buttonJump

Tips

  • Jump on enemies from above to defeat them.
  • Coins are often placed above safe jumps.
  • Take your time near gaps. Falling costs a life.
