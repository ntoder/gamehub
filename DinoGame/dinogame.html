About Dino Run

A little dinosaur runs across the desert at night and day. Jump over cacti and duck under flying birds. The desert speeds up the longer you survive, and the sky switches between day and night as your score grows.

How to play

Space / UpJump
DownDuck
PhoneTap to jump

Tips

  • Birds can fly low, middle or high. Duck only for the low and middle ones.
  • Holding Down while in the air makes you land faster.
  • Groups of three cactuses need an early jump.
