About Color Echo

A memory game of colours and sounds. Watch the four coloured pads light up in a pattern, then repeat it in the same order. Every round adds one more step to the pattern and plays it a little faster. See how long a pattern you can remember.

How to play

Click / tap a colourRepeat the pattern
Q W A SPress a colour with the keyboard

Tips

  • Say the colours out loud in your head as they light up.
  • Each pad has its own sound, so listen as well as watch.
  • Group the pattern into small chunks of three or four.
