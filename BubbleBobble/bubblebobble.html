About Bubble Blaster

Trap the monsters inside bubbles, then pop them for points. Jump between platforms, blow bubbles at the enemies and clear every monster to move to the next level. Watch out: touching a monster costs you a life.

How to play

Tips

  • Trapped monsters float up. Pop several close together for a combo bonus.
  • Pop trapped monsters quickly before they escape and get angry.
  • Collect the fruit that drops for extra points.
