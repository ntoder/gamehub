About Subway Runner

Run down a three-lane railway as fast as you can. Trains come straight at you, so switch lanes to get out of the way, and jump over the red and white barriers. Grab coins for extra points. The longer you survive, the faster the trains come.

How to play

Left / right arrowsChange lane
Up / SpaceJump
PhoneSwipe left, right or up

Tips

  • Look at the far end of the track, not at your runner.
  • Barriers can be jumped, trains cannot. Change lanes for those.
  • A line of coins is often the safest path.
