About Motor Racing

Ride a motorbike down a busy road. Speed up, weave between cars and see how far you can get before you crash. The faster you go, the more points you earn, but there's less time to react.

Tips

  • Speed up on empty stretches and slow down in traffic.
  • Stay near the middle of the road so you can dodge either way.
  • Watch the cars two lanes ahead, not just the next one.
