About Snake

Steer a growing snake around the board and eat the food. Every bite makes the snake longer and adds to your score. Crash into a wall or into your own tail and the game is over.

How to play

Arrow keysChange direction
PauseTake a break

Tips

  • Move along the edges of the board to keep the middle free.
  • Plan your path a few moves ahead once the snake gets long.
  • Never turn back toward your own tail.
