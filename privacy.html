Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

The short version: you can play without an account. If you make one, we keep your name, email, and game scores so we can save your progress. We don't sell your information. Free players see Google ads, which use cookies. You can ask us to delete your account at any time.

1. Who we are

Noam's Game Hub (noamtoder.com) is run by Noam Toder. Contact: noam@rbscode.com.

2. What we collect

  • Account details: when you sign up, your display name and email address. If you sign in with Google, we also get your Google profile picture.
  • Game data: your best score, last score, number of plays and when you last played each game.
  • Account status: whether you have Premium or have asked for it, when you joined and when you last logged in.
  • Saved in your browser: some games keep your best score in your browser's local storage so it shows even when you're not logged in.
  • Usage statistics: Google Analytics records which pages are visited, roughly where visitors are from, and what device they use. It does not tell us who you are.
  • Advertising: visitors without Premium see ads from Google AdSense. Because many of our players are children, our ad requests ask Google to treat visitors as children, so the ads are not personalised based on your interests or browsing. Google may still use cookies to show ads, limit how often you see them and prevent fraud.

We never see or store your password. Sign-in is handled by Google Firebase Authentication.

3. Why we use it

  • To log you in and save your scores and progress.
  • To give Premium members ad-free access and Premium games.
  • To see which games people enjoy and fix problems.
  • To show ads to free players, which keeps the site running.

4. Who can see it

  • You can see your own profile and scores.
  • The site admin (Noam) can see account names, emails, scores and Premium status in order to run the site.
  • Service providers: Google Firebase stores the data (on servers in the United States), Google Analytics and Google AdSense as described above, and GitHub Pages hosts the website.

We don't sell or rent your information, and we don't share it with anyone else unless the law requires it.

5. Children

Many of our players are young. If you are under 13 (or the minimum age for your country), please get a parent's or guardian's permission before making an account. Parents can contact us to see or delete their child's account. You can always play without an account; nothing is saved then except in your own browser.

6. Cookies and choices

  • You can block or clear cookies in your browser settings. The games still work, but you may need to log in again.
  • You can manage Google ad settings at adssettings.google.com.
  • Premium members don't get ads loaded at all.

7. Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your account until you ask us to delete it, or until it has been unused for a long time. To see, correct or delete your data, email noam@rbscode.com from the email on your account, and we'll take care of it within 30 days. You can change your display name yourself on your profile page.

8. Security

Your data is protected by Google Firebase and by database rules that only let you change your own account. No website is 100% secure, so please use a password you don't use anywhere else.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. Big changes will be announced on the site.
