About Maze Muncher

Race through the maze eating every dot while colourful ghosts chase you. Eat all the dots to win the level. If a ghost catches you, you lose a life. You have three lives to clear the board.

How to play

Arrow keysMove through the maze

Tips

  • Clear one area of the maze at a time.
  • Watch where the ghosts are before turning into a long corridor.
  • Leave a few dots near an escape route for the end.
